What you get

  • T. Track Pick one messy workflow that moves end to end. Name when it starts, when done means done, and which systems it touches.
  • R. Record List every human touch between start and done (who, what, channel, minutes). Include checking, nudging, and waiting on a reply.
  • A. Audit Mark where information is copied between systems by hand: paste jobs, re-keying, and status chasing that could be a rule.
